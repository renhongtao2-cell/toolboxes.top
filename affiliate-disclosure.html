Affiliate Disclosure

Last Updated: July 1, 2026

Our Commitment to Transparency

ToolBoxHub believes in full transparency with our users. This disclosure explains our relationships with third-party companies and how we may earn commissions through affiliate links on our website.

Amazon Associates Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. This means that when you click on links to Amazon.com or other affiliated sites on our website and make a purchase, we may receive a small commission at no additional cost to you.

These commissions help us maintain the website, develop new calculators, and continue providing free financial tools to our users.

How Affiliate Links Work

Affiliate links are special tracking URLs that allow us to know when a visitor clicks on a link and makes a purchase. The link contains a unique identifier tied to our account.

When you click on an affiliate link, you may be directed to a third-party website. We only recommend products and services that we believe will provide genuine value to our users.

Our Review Process

Our recommendations are based on thorough research and analysis. We consider factors such as:

  • Product features and functionality
  • User reviews and ratings
  • Price and value for money
  • Customer service reputation
  • Industry standards and best practices

No Influence on Recommendations

Our affiliate relationships do not influence our recommendations. We would recommend a product even if we did not earn a commission from it. Our primary goal is to provide honest, unbiased information to help you make informed financial decisions.

Other Affiliate Programs

In addition to Amazon Associates, we may participate in other affiliate programs. These programs operate similarly, where we earn commissions from qualifying purchases made through our referral links.

Your Trust Matters

We value your trust and want to be completely transparent about our business model. If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships or disclosure practices, please contact us.

Thank you for your support!

Your purchases through our affiliate links help keep ToolBoxHub free and independent.

How We Select and Test Products

Every calculator and tool featured on ToolBoxHub is evaluated using a rigorous testing methodology. We assess accuracy by comparing results against known financial formulas and industry-standard calculators. User experience is tested across multiple devices and browsers to ensure accessibility. We also evaluate the educational value of accompanying content, ensuring each tool includes clear explanations of the underlying mathematics and practical usage tips.

Our testing process involves at least 50 test cases per calculator, covering edge cases like zero balances, negative values, and extremely large numbers. We verify that results match professional financial software within acceptable rounding tolerances. Tools that fail any accuracy check are revised before publication.

Your Privacy and Data Security

All calculations on ToolBoxHub are performed entirely in your browser. We do not store, transmit, or access any financial data you enter into our calculators. Your numbers never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy. We use cookies only for essential site functionality and analytics to improve user experience. We do not sell or share any personal information with third parties.
